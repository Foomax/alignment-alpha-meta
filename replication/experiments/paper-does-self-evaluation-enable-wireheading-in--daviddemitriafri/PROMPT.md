## The claim you are testing

> Training Llama-3.1-8B and Mistral-7B with online RL where models' self-assigned grades directly determine the reward causes rapid grade inflation (models learn to claim near-perfect scores while true task accuracy flatlines or collapses), and this effect is much stronger on ambiguous tasks like summarization than on objective tasks like arithmetic; decoupling the reward from the self-grade largely eliminates the inflation.

Supporting quote from the post: "We found that in the Selfgrade condition, models rapidly learned to saturate the reward signal (claiming perfect scores) while actual task accuracy flatlined or collapsed."

**Headline metric:** grade inflation (self-reported reward minus task accuracy)
**Claimed value:** Selfgrade condition: inflation reaches 0.41 for summarization (reward saturating near 1.0, accuracy plateaus around 0.5); Honest condition: near-zero inflation (range -0.02 to +0.03)
**Direction of the claim:** +
**Uncertainty the post reported:** none
**Tolerance for "reproduced":** `manual` — headline is a compound of 5 numbers; compare the components by hand
